What a home costs after the offer is accepted.

Condo Holding Cost estimates the carrying cost of a Canadian condo, detached house, or townhouse. A realtor can walk through the numbers with a client. The figures stay on the phone.

Three kinds of home

Condo

Economic holding cost for this year and a year you choose: mortgage interest, property tax, strata, insurance, utilities, a special-levy reserve, and the opportunity cost of cash. Principal is treated as equity, not as a cost. A rental can show tax savings. A principal residence does not deduct those costs each year.

Detached house

Annual and monthly cash flow the buyer actually pays, including principal. Property tax, water, sewer, garbage, full-building insurance, energy, and a maintenance reserve. A common starting point is about 1% of the price a year, or $1–$2 per square foot, until you replace it with real bills.

Townhouse

The same cash flow, plus strata fees and what those fees include: heat, hot water, gas, water, or gardening. A thin contingency reserve raises the special-levy reserve. The owner’s policy is treated as interior, contents, and the building deductible, because the strata often insures the shell.

How to use it

1. Start from the list

Tap +, then choose a condo, a detached house, or a townhouse.

The library stays on the device. Export JSON if you want the same file on another phone.

2. Place the condo

For a condo, set the place, the price, and either a mortgage or a cash purchase. Check strata, insurance, and utilities.

Turn financing off when the buyer is paying cash.

3. Read the holding cost

Read this year’s holding cost and a later year. Export the PDF if the client should keep a copy.

Read the Bank of Canada policy rate, prime, the 5-year bond, and the posted 5-year mortgage when the phone is online. A discounted mortgage starts from the bond plus 1.5 percentage points.

4. Place the house

For a detached house or townhouse, find it on the map, by address, or by postal code. The app fills a tax rate, heating degree days, and a townhouse fee estimate. Change any of those, then enter the tax notice and the last 12 months of bills when you have them.

For a townhouse, enter the strata fee, what it includes, the reserve ratio, and the unit count. Read the depreciation report and minutes before you trust a low fee.

Mark roof, heating, age, and hazards such as a floodplain, wildfire interface, aluminum wiring, or Poly-B. Use an insurance quote when you have one.

5. Read the cash flow

Read the annual cash flow, the monthly cash flow, and the lender housing cost. Add income only if you want GDS and TDS.

For a detached house or townhouse, the app also shows a monthly housing cost in the shape used for gross debt service: principal and interest, property tax, heat, and half the strata fee. If you enter household income and other monthly debt, it shows GDS and TDS. The stress-test payment uses the contract rate plus 2 percentage points, and at least 5.25%. The often-cited 39% and 44% guides are labels, not an approval.

Share a detached or townhouse estimate as text.

6. Sit with the client

On a wide or folded screen, one side keeps the inputs and the other side shows the same condo, detached, or townhouse result.

The interface follows the iPhone language: Simplified Chinese, English, or Canadian French.

What this is not

It is not a lender, an insurer, an appraiser, or a tax opinion. City tax rates shipped with the app are reference points. Winnipeg’s figure is an effective-rate estimate. Insurance without a quote is a range from house type, roof, and hazards. Energy without bills is a model from floor area, heating degree days, age, and the heating system. Replace those with the tax notice, the strata documents, and the utility bills before you rely on them.

Estimates for a conversation with a client. Not a commitment to lend.
