Condo Holding Cost
Product Privacy

14 November 2026

Privacy

Condo Holding Cost is an estimator that runs on your iPhone. The developer does not operate an account system and does not receive a copy of the homes you enter.

What stays on the device

Names, prices, mortgages, taxes, strata fees, insurance figures, utility amounts, income if you type it, notes, and map coordinates are saved in a JSON file in the app’s local storage. Deleting a home removes it from that file. Deleting the app removes the file. Export writes a JSON or PDF only when you ask, and you choose where it goes. Import reads a file you pick. An import updates matching records and does not delete the other homes already on the phone.

Location

The app asks for location only when you tap the control that uses your current position. That coordinate can be saved as the condo’s location. The developer does not receive it. Place search uses Apple Maps. Apple processes that search under Apple’s privacy policy.

Network requests

When the phone is online, the app requests public series from the Bank of Canada Valet API and a consumer-price series from Statistics Canada. About every two weeks it also reads published residential tax-rate pages from city websites such as Toronto, Calgary, and Vancouver. A rate is saved on the device only when that page contains a residential total the app can read. Those requests do not include your homes, your name, or your location. There is no developer analytics kit, no advertising kit, and no account login.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not sell personal information. It is not directed at children.

What you share

A PDF, a JSON export, or the system share sheet sends information only to the destination you choose, such as Mail or Files. The developer is not a party to that transfer.

Your choices

You can decline location permission and still type an address or a postal code. You can leave income blank. You can delete individual homes or delete the app. You can export the library and keep the file yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. The copy published with the App Store listing is the one that applies to that version of the app.

Contact

Use the support link on the App Store product page. Do not send account passwords or full identity documents.
